# M2 phase 1: Flag Game at N=16 (2026-10-06)

Purpose: validate the framework end to end with real models. Not an experimental result; one Haiku seed, three Qwen seeds, prompts that never mention the board.

## Framework findings
- Both providers ran at N=16 with zero provider errors and zero failed tool calls (465 Haiku calls; >2,000 Qwen calls).
- Replay of a real run: 0.2 s, zero provider calls. Fork of a real run at round 5: 614-event prefix byte-identical, live continuation diverged at round 8.
- Viewer on a 16-agent LLM run: 369 KB, no JS errors; the per-turn view shows agents reading `collective_status` and switching guesses after reading a post.
- Sequential commit is impractical with a high-latency provider; per-request timeouts (90 s, 2 retries) were added but the policy belongs with fast providers or small N.
- Probe-sourced metrics were not declared in the spec (computed offline instead); the spec needs a string shorthand.
- Costs: Haiku $3.46 per 16×10 run (probes 29% of it); Qwen $0.25. The flat estimate overstated Haiku 4×.

## Behavioural observations (one seed for Haiku; treat as hypotheses)
- Haiku converges to full consensus on the truth by round 3, but mostly by reading `collective_status` (guess counts) and following the majority; posts are rare (~1 per round swarm-wide). With 81% of agents correct from crops alone, majority-following works here; it would herd onto a wrong majority just as efficiently.
- Qwen3.5-9B does not communicate unprompted: board reads on <4% of turns, 94–97% of agents never read, almost no posts; accuracy stays at 0.5–0.69 and in two runs a large block (38%, 50%) settled on the rival. ~4% of turns truncated at the token limit even with thinking off.
- Probe answers track committed guesses closely (disagreement ≤ 6%).

## Candidate phase-2 arms
1. `bc-haiku` with `status_tools: []` (no collective_status): does consensus still form, and through what channel?
2. `bc-haiku` with a system prompt that mentions the board exists (explicit experimental condition).
3. A larger open model on the router (Qwen3.8-27B) in place of 9B: does size restore unprompted communication?
4. N=64 Haiku broadcast, one seed: the scaling check (gate concurrency, inbox fan-out, prompt growth).
5. Paired runs from round 0 with one crop swapped, plus an unchanged pair, for the influence metric.
